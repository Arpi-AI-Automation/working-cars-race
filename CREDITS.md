## Vehicles

Kenney **Car Kit** 3.1 (ambulance, firetruck, garbage-truck, tractor, truck), downloaded from <https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit>.

- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- The pack says you may copy, modify, and use the models for any purpose, including commercial projects. Credit is not required. This game credits Kenney anyway.
- A copy of the pack's `License.txt` is in `public/models/vehicles/License.txt`.
- The GLB files reference `Textures/colormap.png` beside them. That shared texture from the pack is included so the vehicles keep their colors.
- Garage icons are the pack's 64×64 previews.
